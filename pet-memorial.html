What we review

Practical details that affect the finished product

Material route

Supplier-declared base material, surface finish, plating route, and available documentation.

Construction

Jump rings, chain connection, screw thread, closure, sealing parts, engraving area, and assembly.

Presentation

Pouch, box, insert card, labeling, visible defects, and sample-to-bulk consistency points.

Clear boundaries

Product sourcing and development, without hidden service claims

These boundaries keep the project practical for both the buyer and supplier.

  • We do not handle ashes, hair, resin filling, or end-customer personalization.
  • Product availability, MOQ, price, and lead time are confirmed only after supplier review.
  • Material and compliance documents are requested from suppliers or laboratories; we do not self-certify.
  • Buyer references remain the basis for development. Similar public images are used only as direction.
